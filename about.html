Let’s Talk

Designing clear digital products, from research to prototype.

I’m Salvador Gonzalez Alvizouri; Sal for short, a UX/UI and product designer based in Jalisco, Mexico. Starting out in art direction and agency web design, I transitioned into UX/UI in 2018 with a focus on turning complex web tools into clear, intuitive, and well crafted products.

Today, I lead product and design across permissions, information architecture, and design systems. I enjoy shaping the bigger picture while staying hands on with the details using AI to prototype and validate ideas rapidly before they ever reach a developer.

Outside of work, I’m drawn to art, cinema, gastronomy, and travel. Exploring new places, films, and food culture keeps me curious and continually feeds the visual sensibility I bring back to my design work.

My process

  1. 01

    Research & Understand

    I start with the people and the product: interviews, store reviews, hands-on use of the existing app and what the business needs. The goal is to name the real problem before I draw anything.

  2. 02

    Ideating & Testing

    I turn what I learned into flows, sketches and low-fidelity wireframes, and show them early to the team and to users, so wrong turns are cheap to fix.

  3. 03

    Designing & Prototyping

    I design the interface and its system in Figma, then prototype the key flows, using AI to move faster, so ideas can be tried before a developer builds them.

  4. 04

    Testing & Launching

    I check the near-final design for usability and accessibility, hand developers clear specs and stay close until it ships.
