I started in art direction, so I care how things look. Years later, I care more about how they’re structured.

I’m Salvador Gonzalez Alvizouri; Sal for short, a product designer based in Jalisco, Mexico. Starting out in art direction and agency web design, I transitioned into UX/UI in 2018 with a focus on turning complex web tools into clear, intuitive, and well crafted products.

Today, I lead product and design across permissions, information architecture, and design systems. I enjoy shaping the bigger picture while staying hands on with the details using AI to prototype and validate ideas rapidly before they ever reach a developer.

Outside of work, I’m drawn to art, cinema, gastronomy, and travel. Exploring new places, films, and food culture keeps me curious and continually feeds the visual sensibility I bring back to my design work.

Algovisual is the name I work under: my two surnames, Alvizouri and Gonzalez, put together.

Three rules I work by

  1. Structure before screens

    I map who sees what before I draw anything.

  2. Users before polish

    Real walkthroughs caught mistakes in my own first version.

  3. Testing for improvement

    Iterating and adjusting is the only way to keep improving a product.
